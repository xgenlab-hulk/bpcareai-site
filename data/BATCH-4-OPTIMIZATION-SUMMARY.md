## Optimization Patterns & Best Practices

### Slug Optimization Patterns

**Effective Abbreviations:**
- TRT (testosterone replacement therapy)
- BP (blood pressure)
- HRV (heart rate variability)
- GLP-1 (GLP-1 agonist)
- MCI (mild cognitive impairment)
- CAD (coronary artery disease)
- IBS-D (IBS with diarrhea)
- HbA1c → A1c (in titles for user-friendliness)

**Words Removed:**
- Filler prefixes: "5-things-", "12-science-backed-ways-to-"
- Unnecessary qualifiers: "especially", "particularly", "safe-for"
- Redundant terms: "diabetes" when context clear from insulin/blood sugar
- Verbose phrases: "should-know-before", "that-work-for"

**Kept for Clarity:**
- Age specifications: 50s, 55-64, 65+, 70s, 74+
- Medical conditions: alzheimers, hashimoto, stenosis
- Action context: holiday, potluck, buffet, party

### Title Optimization Patterns

**Question Formats That Worked Well:**
- "X Raising Your Y?" (personal, engaging)
- "X After Y?" (temporal, relevant)
- "X Making You Y?" (cause-effect, emotional)
- "Can't X?" (frustration, empathy)
- "Your X Doing Y?" (ownership, concern)

**Emotional Hooks:**
- "Worrying You?" → "Getting Worse?"
- "Can't Stop?" → "Struggling?"
- "Safe?" → "Risky?"
- "Normal?" → "Hidden Problem?"

### Description Optimization Patterns

**Strong Opening Hooks:**
- "Worried about X?" (empathy)
- "New diagnosis + X?" (relevance)
- "X despite Y?" (contradiction)
- "Managing X?" (active engagement)

**Quantified Value Propositions:**
- "5 proven strategies" (specific number + credibility)
- "3 tests Medicare covers" (actionable + practical)
- "Cut sodium 40%" (precise benefit)
- "Reduce X by 18%" (measurable outcome)

**Strong CTAs:**
- "Discover" (exploration)
- "Get" (acquisition)
- "Proven" (credibility)
- "Essential" (urgency)
- "Stop" (prevention)

### PrimaryKeyword Optimization Patterns

**Before → After Examples:**
- "family history high blood pressure home monitoring" → "family history high blood pressure home monitoring 50s"
- "holiday meals dysphagia swallowing seniors" → "holiday dysphagia swallowing safety seniors"
- "testosterone therapy insulin resistance men 65" → "testosterone therapy insulin resistance men"

**Key Principles:**
- Remove unnecessary prepositions ("of", "for", "with")
- Add age/demographic when it strengthens search intent
- Remove age when already in other metadata fields (avoid redundancy)
- Use user-friendly terms over academic jargon
- Maintain natural search phrasing
